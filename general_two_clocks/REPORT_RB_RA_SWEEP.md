## Reading

- The Taylor-microscale ratio is the small-scale operator-split diagnostic: pressure's smallest active scale vs buoyancy's. It stays well above 1 at every Ra and grows as Ra increases and buoyancy plumes sharpen, while the inverse-Laplacian keeps pressure smooth: the split does not close at higher Ra; it widens.
- The integral-length ratio stays near 1 at every Ra: both fields share the convection-roll wavelength. The split lives at small scales only.
- The high-k suppression factor (buoyancy power over pressure power in the top half-decade of k_x) is the 1/k^2 elliptic-filter footprint.
